# YapiTech Engineering Handbook

> **Engineering software that evolves with the business.**

---

## Welcome

Welcome to the official **YapiTech Engineering Handbook**.

This handbook defines the engineering philosophy, principles, standards, architectural practices, and development guidelines followed across every software product developed by YapiTech.

It serves as the single source of truth for how we design, build, document, and maintain software.

Whether developing a new SaaS platform, enhancing an existing product, or contributing to open-source initiatives, every engineer should understand and follow the practices documented here.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this handbook is to ensure that software engineering at YapiTech remains:

- Consistent
- Maintainable
- Scalable
- Well documented
- Business focused
- Adaptable to future change

Rather than relying on personal preferences or undocumented practices, engineering decisions are guided by documented principles and standards that evolve alongside the company.

---

# Engineering Philosophy

At YapiTech, we believe that software should model real businesses—not simply user interfaces or database tables.

Every architectural decision should make software easier to understand, extend, and maintain throughout its lifetime.

Our philosophy can be summarised as:

> **We design software that models real businesses, preserves history, adapts to change, and communicates through well-defined business events. Every architectural decision should reduce future complexity rather than introduce it.**

# Engineering Workflow

Every significant feature developed at YapiTech follows the same engineering workflow.

1. Understand the business requirement.
2. Design the solution.
3. Model the data.
4. Design the API.
5. Implement the solution.
6. Test the feature.
7. Document important decisions.
8. Refactor when improvements are identified.

Documentation grows alongside the software.

---

# Documentation Philosophy

Documentation is considered part of the product.

Engineering knowledge should never exist only inside an engineer's memory.

Whenever an important architectural or engineering decision is made, it should be documented for future engineers.

Every engineer contributing to a YapiTech product commits to building software that:

- Models the business before the technology.
- Preserves important business history.
